# HSK Words Visualized

This public web page helps learners explore vocabulary from the old HSK program, covering **HSK 1, HSK 2, HSK 3, HSK 4, HSK 5, and HSK 6**.

Instead of reading vocabulary as a plain list, the page presents the words in a visual and interactive space so learners can browse, review, and remember them more easily.

![HSK Words Visualized](assets/images/hsk-words-visualized_page_1.png)

## Key Features

- Covers the old HSK 1-6 vocabulary set.
- Supports direct interaction inside the vocabulary interface.
- Supports **zoom** for focusing on a specific area.
- Supports **pan** for moving around the vocabulary map or overview.
- Shows Chinese characters, pinyin, English meanings, and Vietnamese meanings.
- Useful for review, quick lookup, and seeing how vocabulary is organized across levels.

## How To Use

Open the public page with the **OPEN CONTENT** button on the card. Once inside, use zoom and pan to move through the vocabulary space, then inspect each word or cluster to view its details.

The goal is to make HSK study less dry: Chinese characters, pinyin, English meaning, and Vietnamese meaning stay close together in one visual interface for quick comparison.
